Source: `PLAN-final-plan.md` §5 WP1.7, R5, R7, A11, A13; `PLAN.md` §4.3; `research/03-agent-sessions.md` and
`research/01-local-findings.md` (Claude sections). Every command is read-only: `ls`, `stat`, `jq` over files that match
`^[0-9]+\.json$`, `ps -o … -p <pid>`, `claude agents --json` and `claude --version`. No `*.key` file was opened (they
were only counted by name), no socket was opened and no session was started. Values here are field names, counts,
versions and shapes: no session names, paths, ids or tokens. Raw captures sit under the gitignored
`target/fixture-staging/claude-registry/` and `target/fixture-staging/claude/`; the committed rows in
`tests/fixtures/claude/registry/` went through `scripts/sanitize_fixture.py` after pids, ids, names and paths were
replaced. The transcript line types, the task-list location (R7) and `history.jsonl` belong to the second half of
WP1.7 (step 12) and are not covered here.
