# App window-title formats and unread markers, revalidation 2026-10-06 (WP5.3 first tier, A15)

Source: `PLAN-final-plan.md` §5 WP5.3 ("title formats on current app versions, including cmux titles and the A15
unread markers", "every `[U]` finding"), A15, §4.4; `research/04-app-activity.md` (per-app sections);
`docs/spikes/0003-cmux.md` question 4.

Every probe is one read-only `aerospace list-windows --monitor all --json --format '%{window-id} %{app-bundle-id}
%{app-name} %{window-title}'`, saved under `target/fixture-staging/titles/` (gitignored) and inspected through a
masking script that replaces every word with a placeholder and keeps only separators, brackets, dashes and counts.
No app was started, focused or scripted, and no AppleScript ran. Nothing here quotes a real title: formats use
`<field>` placeholders. At run time there were 29 windows of 12 bundle ids. Installed versions: Chrome 154.0.8037.98,
Slack 4.45.69, Teams 26213.1006.5011.1671, Outlook 16.108.1, cmux 0.64.25, Obsidian 1.7.4 (installed, not running),
JetBrains IDEs with 2026.x configuration directories (20 JetBrains windows: 10 IntelliJ IDEA, 9 PyCharm, 1 DataGrip).

The sanitized fixtures are `tests/fixtures/appcontext/titles/<app>.json` (each item `{bundle_id, title, expected}`)
and `tests/fixtures/appcontext/unread/<app>.json` (each item `{bundle_id, title, unread}`). They keep the observed
format and replace content with `customer-1`, `ticket-123`, `notes.md` and `@example.com` addresses.
